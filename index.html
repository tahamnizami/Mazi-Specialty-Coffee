A place for slower moments

Simple coffee. Real conversation.

Mazi is inspired by the feeling of simpler times — a small place where the pace drops, the music feels warm, and conversation takes center stage. It is not about luxury. It is about atmosphere, comfort, and the kind of lingering that makes a day feel better.

At Mazi, coffee is part of the story, but the real experience is in the space: the people, the music, the open-roof evenings, and the ease of just being present.

More Than Coffee

There is more to a good evening than a good cup.

Mazi was designed with a simple idea in mind: put the screen away, listen to the music, talk to the person across from you, and let the evening breathe.

There is no Wi‑Fi here by design. Put the screen away, listen to the music, talk to the person sitting across from you, and enjoy the moment.

The experience is calm and human. No rush. No clutter. Just a warm place to slow down and share a little time.

Rules & Regulation

A few things to keep in mind

Outside Food & Drinks: No outside food or drinks.
Cleanliness & Decorum: Please maintain cleanliness and decorum throughout the space.
Gatherings & Parties: Please inform us in advance for gatherings and parties.
Photography & Shoots: Approval in advance is required for any kind of shoot, personal or professional.
Minimum Spending: There is no minimum spending at Mazi. However, if you are sitting for hours, please at least order a coffee.
Collaborations: We do not entertain paid collaborations or barter collaborations of any kind.
Respect the Vibe: Please respect the vibe and the place.
Children & Families: Children and families are requested to ensure that children do not run around or disturb other guests.
Every brick and corner holds a story. Please treat it with the same warmth and respect it was built with.
